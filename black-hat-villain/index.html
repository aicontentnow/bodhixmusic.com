Victoria, BC · 2009 to 2011

Black Hat Villain

Five-piece hard rock with Scotty Tuesday out front.

The story

After SEASØNS, life moved back to Victoria. Friends from the old neighbourhood had a band and needed a singer, and Scotty Tuesday stepped up front in 2009.

My Generation, the EP, was cut at Infinity Studios in Victoria with Adam Sutherland. It carried the band into the finals of 99.3 The Fox's Seeds competition, where they finished second, onto the stage at the Commodore, and out on the road around BC and Alberta.

Hard rock is hard on a voice. In April 2011 Scotty stepped away to put family and steady work first. No hard feelings. The band played on.
