Where SEASØNS began.

The story

SEASONS was written, played, produced, recorded, mixed and designed alone in a Vancouver apartment between 2006 and 2008, while falling in love. Fifty-seven songs across one year, in four seasons.

It came out in 2008 as Scott Valentine Presents: five discs, one CD for each season, plus a DVD short film, Seasons: A Film Journey, that told the story with one song from each season.

With a manager on board came a website and a song a week for a year. Free downloads went out on little cards embedded with seeds, each with a QR code: plant the card, keep the song. Radio stations picked it up, and the walk around Vancouver handing them out became a video.

In 2019 the four seasons were remastered and released as Scotty Tuesday. The five-disc sets are collector's items now. Today BODHI X is singing these songs again: enter SEASØNS.
