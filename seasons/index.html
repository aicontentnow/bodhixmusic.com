The ring

The ring is the year. 56 marks sit inside it, one per song, in the order they come out. They light as each one arrives.

How to play

Drag around the circle to wind the year. Let go and it stays there a while, then settles back to Spring, or to today's season when Today is on. Drag anywhere else to blow wind through the branch.
