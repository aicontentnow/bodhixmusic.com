A Vancouver trio. Two records, made before YouTube and all of that.

The story

After Heavy Sun, the search for a drummer turned up Clayton Wolsey. With Keith Trusler on bass, the three of them became the next t‑shirt.

The first record took ten months, made in the drummer's apartment on the Lower East Side of Vancouver. The Fifth Floor came two years later, five songs cut at the Warehouse Studios, and it was the last thing the three of them recorded together.
