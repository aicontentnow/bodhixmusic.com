Heavy Sun

The first band. Started at 19.

The story

Heavy Sun started in Victoria in 1994, with Scott Valentine on vocals, lyrics and guitar. For eight years the band wrote its own songs and recorded them itself.

In 1998 Scott and guitarist Jason Jarvis moved to Vancouver to chase music full time. The search for a new drummer led to Clayton Wolsey, and that led to the next band, The Next T‑Shirt.

Greatest Hits is an unofficial look back at those years: fifteen songs, no official artwork, no official release. Just where it all started.
