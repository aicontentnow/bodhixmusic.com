The story

After Black Hat Villain came a move to Vancouver, a job in marketing, and a long stretch with the music packed away. The songs kept coming anyway.

Unreleased Gems gathers forty-eight of them: pre-production demos and songs that were lost, forgotten, cut from earlier records or never funded. Some were made in a studio, some were caught on the spot. Most are solo, with a few friends along the way.

Many are early takes of SEASØNS songs, the same ones BODHI X is singing again now: Watch It Grow, Cheerleader, Jukebox, Freak In You, Rewind, Believe In This. There are more still to add.
