Privacy

This is the home of BODHI X, one songwriter's music. Here is everything the site keeps track of, in plain words.

What gets counted

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits, so I can see whether the music is finding people. It records:

  • which pages are opened, and for how long
  • roughly where a visit comes from: country and city, never an address
  • the kind of device and browser
  • which link or post brought you here
  • taps on the pre-save, listen and follow buttons, and which songs are played on the site and for how long

Google Analytics sets a small cookie so a second visit counts as a returning one. It does not tell me who you are, and I never see your name, email or IP address.

The newsletter

If you sign up, your email address is kept by Kit, the newsletter service, and used only to send BODHI X news. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works straight away. Your address is never sold or shared.

What never happens

  • No ads and no ad tracking on this site.
  • Nothing about you is sold or traded.
  • Reports only ever show totals, never one person.

BODHI X on social apps

BODHI X reads the statistics of its own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts (views, followers, likes and similar totals) to see what is working. That reading uses only those accounts' own figures. It never reads anyone else's account, messages or contacts, and it never posts.

Opting out

Blocking cookies, or using a content blocker or Google's own opt-out add-on, stops your visit being counted. The site works the same either way.
